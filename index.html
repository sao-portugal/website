Rooftops. Beach clubs. Long summer nights.

Active days.
Social nights.

We're two friends in Lisbon who like to keep moving. Surfing, training, cycling, walking the hills all day. And at the end of it, we love a drink with friends just as much. We wanted one that fits that life: light, cold, nothing heavy. Portugal's bars and beach clubs weren't pouring it yet. So we made it.

São is Portuguese for healthy, whole. It's how we like to live: busy days, good company, long evenings.
