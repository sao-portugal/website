FAQ.

How many followers do I need?

None in particular. We care more about your style and your audience than the number. Most of your followers should be 18+.

Does it cost anything?

No. Joining is free, always.

Do I have to post a set amount?

At level 1 there are no fixed quotas. The more you create, the faster you level up. Paid contracts at level 3 come with clear, agreed deliverables.

When do I get paid?

Commission on your code starts once our online shop opens. Paid contracts are agreed one-on-one with our top creators.

I don't live in Lisbon. Can I still apply?

Yes. We start in Lisbon and the Algarve, but creators anywhere in Portugal are welcome to apply.

Can I stop whenever I want?

Of course. No lock-ins at levels 1 and 2. Just let us know.
